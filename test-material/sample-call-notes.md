# Call notes: Brightwave Software, discovery call

Fictional prospect, for testing only.

**Attendees:** Priya Shah (CRO), Tom Ellis (Head of Sales Ops)

- About 80 account executives selling into mid-market finance teams. Average deal is around $85k a year.
- Business cases are "a mess". Every AE builds their own spreadsheet. Tom thinks it takes 6 hours per
  business case, and each AE does about 2 a month.
- Priya: "We lose too many deals at the CFO. They don't believe our numbers." Thinks around 40% of
  late-stage deals stall at finance review.
- Win rate is about 22%. The board wants it closer to 30% next year.
- No consistent value messaging. New AEs take about 6 months to ramp, and value selling is the
  hardest part.
- They tried a value consultancy two years ago. It was useful, but it faded after the consultants left.
- Already pay for Claude Team for the whole sales org. IT pushes back on new tools: "anything new
  takes a quarter to get through security".
- Next step: Tom to share their current business case template. Follow-up call in two weeks.
